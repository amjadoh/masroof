# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People reviewing their personal finances from Saudi banking SMS on Android, in Arabic or English.

## Product Purpose

Turn original banking evidence into a traceable ledger, owned assets and useful financial summaries. Masroof 2 is an isolated Beta entered from production developer settings, with its own optional historical import and explicit financial approval.

## Brand Personality

Clear, familiar and evidence-led. The approved production Masroof interface is the visual reference.

## Anti-references

An alternative experimental dashboard, invented balances, automatic ownership guesses, and application maintenance controls mixed into ordinary financial settings.

## Design Principles

- Reuse production display components and the shared Masroof tokens.
- Keep financial interpretation, ownership, approval and display separate.
- Show unavailable evidence explicitly rather than inventing zero.
- Apply drafts only through explicit confirmation; preserve data on cancellation and failure.
- Keep production and Beta data independent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve Arabic RTL and English layouts, light and dark themes, large text, accessible labels and adaptive phone/foldable layouts. Use shared typography, semantic colors and touch targets.
